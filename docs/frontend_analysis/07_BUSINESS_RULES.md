## Official guidance checks and unresolved tax questions

The current [HMRC simplified vehicle expenses guidance](https://www.gov.uk/simpler-income-tax-simplified-expenses/vehicles) supports the UI's 55p first-band rate for 2026-27, distinguishes earlier years, excludes certain commercial-use cars including black cabs/hackney carriages, and restricts prior-claimed vehicles. This makes eligibility a critical gap because DrivingView offers Hackney and ClaimMethodView offers mileage without screening. Preserve rule-year and official-source evidence rather than correcting 55p to an older remembered rate. Verify actual-cost-to-mileage transitions and vehicle history before enforcing the UI's two-way permanent-lock wording.

[HMRC payments-on-account guidance](https://www.gov.uk/understand-self-assessment-bill/payments-on-account) includes an exception for substantial tax paid outside Self Assessment. The UI's simple bill-over-1000 explanation omits that context, particularly relevant to the PAYE persona.

The phase thresholds in MtdView broadly align with the checked [official MTD eligibility guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax). Eligibility still depends on qualifying-income years, business types and exemptions, not forecast alone. Verify actual HMRC obligation periods/payloads, authorization, recognition and acknowledgement handling before enabling submissions; a combined taxi/property preview does not establish one remote submission contract.

Other visible values remain UNVERIFIED design assumptions: Personal Allowance 12570 GBP, higher-rate boundary 50270 GBP, 20/40% income bands, Class4 6/2%, Class2 wording, 1000 GBP trading allowance, VAT90000 threshold/rolling-period tests, student-loan plans, Marriage Allowance1260/252, receipt retention until31 Jan2033 and tax dates. Validate Scotland, residency, losses, adjustments, age/NI exceptions, VAT status and relevant tax year. Record source URL, verification timestamp and accountant-approved rule version in tax_rule_sets; no official-looking UI text substitutes for this work.
